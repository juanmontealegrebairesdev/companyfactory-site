CompanyFactory

A venture studio that tests startup ideas against real market signal.

CompanyFactory programmatically launches landing pages and paid-search campaigns to validate early-stage ideas. We measure real demand — clicks, signups, leads — and let the data decide which ventures to scale, iterate, or kill.

How we use Google Ads

For each venture we incubate, we publish a dedicated landing page and run targeted Search campaigns against a small, bounded budget. The goal is to measure intent at the query level: which keywords convert, at what cost-per-acquisition, and whether the idea clears our internal viability thresholds.

  • One Google Ads sub-account per venture, managed under a single MCC.
  • Search campaigns only, launched paused, with strict per-venture daily budgets.
  • Read-side reporting is pulled via GAQL for cost, impressions, clicks, and conversions.
  • Spend is governed by an internal budget engine before any campaign goes live.
